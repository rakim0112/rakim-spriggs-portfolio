Data & Privacy

ScoutBoard Central is a private-workspace basketball scouting tool. The website may be publicly accessible, but scouting records are not made publicly searchable or viewable by default.

What the app stores

Sign-in email and account identifiers, team workspace membership, roster and game information, observations, event tags, scouting evaluations, and statistics entered by scouts. ScoutBoard may contain youth athlete data: enter only information that you are authorized to collect and share within your team.

Authentication and scouting records are processed through Supabase; this website is delivered through Netlify. Unsubmitted notes and evaluations may be stored in your device's browser storage and are not automatically synced between devices.

Who can access it

Signed-in users can create their own private workspaces and share invitation codes. Workspace members can access reports belonging to that workspace. Team owners manage membership. A workspace owner should revoke access if a scout leaves their organization.

Any scout with workspace access may export the team's content. Do not forward reports, share invitation links publicly, or distribute photos and recordings without appropriate authorization.

Safety and responsible use

Do not upload medical records, precise student contact details, private educational records, passwords, or other highly sensitive personal information. Record basketball observations fairly and distinguish observed stats from official box scores. Obtain required consent and permissions for video, images, or student data. Laws and school policies may impose additional obligations.

Data control and limitations

Player records and notes are held in a cloud database and restricted by team membership controls. Authorized users can download reports, CSV files, and JSON backups. A saved draft on one device is not a guaranteed cloud backup. You should export backups regularly and keep them encrypted or in a secure location.

Public beta features, availability, backup retention, or authentication emails may change. This notice does not establish that ScoutBoard complies with every law or institutional privacy requirement. Operators planning wider adoption should establish a formal privacy policy, retention schedule, consent process, and support contact.
